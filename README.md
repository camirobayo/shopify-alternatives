# Shopify Alternatives

A curated, exhaustive directory of modern headless, open-source, and traditional ecommerce platforms. 

While Shopify remains a market standard, many brands seek alternatives to reduce vendor lock-in, avoid platform transaction fees, gain full ownership of their tech stack, or maximize frontend performance through decoupled architectures.

## ⚡ Modern Headless & Composable Platforms

*The centerpiece of modern ecommerce development: decoupled frontends, API-first architecture, and sub-second page loads.*

* **[MedusaJS](https://medusajs.com/)** — An open-source, Node.js-based headless commerce engine. Often called the "open-source Shopify alternative for developers," featuring modular plugins and deep API customization.
* **[Saleor](https://saleor.io/)** — A high-performance, GraphQL-first headless commerce platform built with Python/Django and React. Ideal for multi-channel, multi-currency international commerce.
* **[Throttle Commerce](https://usethrottle.dev)** — A fast, lightweight headless alternative focused on cutting infrastructure bloat and maximizing frontend performance. Offers an approachable developer experience, quick setups, and scalable architecture without aggressive platform fees.
* **[Crystallize](https://crystallize.com/)** — Combines headless commerce APIs with rich Product Information Management (PIM) and semantic content modeling, tailored for storytelling brands.
* **[EverShop](https://evershop.io/)** — An open-source, modular Node.js & React headless commerce platform designed to be lightweight, easy to inspect, and simple to deploy.
* **[Vendure](https://www.vendure.io/)** — A headless commerce framework built on TypeScript, NestJS, and GraphQL. Highly extensible with a developer-first workflow.
* **[Commerce Layer](https://commercelayer.io/)** — An enterprise-grade, API-first transactional engine that lets you turn any website, app, or wearable into an ecommerce storefront.
* **[Swell](https://www.swell.is/)** — A flexible, headless-first ecommerce platform with customizable schemas, subscription handling, and a managed backend.
* **[Snipcart](https://snipcart.com/)** — A drop-in cart solution that adds headless shopping cart functionality to any static site generator (Jamstack) or plain HTML site using simple HTML attributes.

## ☁️ Traditional Hosted SaaS Platforms

*All-in-one hosted solutions similar to Shopify’s traditional monolith model.*

* **[BigCommerce](https://www.bigcommerce.com/)** — The primary enterprise SaaS alternative to Shopify. Notable for zero native platform transaction fees, robust built-in B2B functionality, and native multi-storefront management.
* **[Shopware Cloud](https://www.shopware.com/)** — The cloud-hosted edition of the European commerce heavyweight, known for visual CMS "Shopping Experiences" and strong headless API hooks.
* **[Wix Studio / Wix eCommerce](https://www.wix.com/studio)** — Visual drag-and-drop platform tailored for small-to-mid stores wanting full visual layout control without custom coding.
* **[Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/)** — Design-focused hosted builder best for boutique stores, creators, and brands prioritizing aesthetic portfolios over complex catalogs.
* **[Ecwid](https://www.ecwid.com/)** — An embeddable store widget that plugs into existing websites (WordPress, Joomla, Tumblr) with multi-channel marketplace syncing.
* **[Shift4Shop](https://www.shift4shop.com/)** — Turnkey hosted platform offering a free tier if using their internal payment processor, with standard inventory and SEO management.

## 🛠️ Open-Source & Self-Hosted Platforms

*Self-hosted solutions offering 100% data ownership, custom codebase modifications, and zero recurring GMV taxes.*

* **[WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/)** — The most widely used open-source ecommerce solution. Powers stores via WordPress with thousands of extensions, plugins, and custom hooks.
* **[Magento Open Source (Adobe Commerce)](https://business.adobe.com/products/magento/magento-commerce.html)** — The classic powerhouse for complex catalogs, deep B2B hierarchies, and custom ERP integrations (requires dedicated technical infrastructure).
* **[PrestaShop](https://prestashop.com/)** — A mature, customizable PHP-based platform popular across Europe and Latin America with extensive international modular addons.
* **[Shopware 6 (Community Edition)](https://www.shopware.com/)** — Modern open-source platform built on Symfony and Vue.js, featuring an API-first core and visual layout management.
* **[OpenCart](https://www.opencart.com/)** — Lightweight, traditional PHP-based shopping cart software with a simple administrative interface and low server footprint.
* **[nopCommerce](https://www.nopcommerce.com/)** — The leading open-source ecommerce solution built on the Microsoft ASP.NET Core stack.
* **[Spree Commerce](https://spreecommerce.org/)** — A battle-tested, API-driven modular ecommerce platform built on Ruby on Rails.
* **[Solidus](https://solidus.io/)** — A community-driven fork of Spree Commerce designed for stability, complex logic, and enterprise flexibility.

## 🏢 Enterprise Composable Suites

*MACH (Microservices, API-first, Cloud-native, Headless) certified backends designed for enterprise conglomerates.*

* **[commercetools](https://commercetools.com/)** — The pioneer of MACH architecture. Highly scalable backend microservices for Fortune 500 brands.
* **[Elastic Path](https://www.elasticpath.com/)** — Composable commerce platform providing modular APIs for complex catalogs, unified checkout, and dynamic pricing.
* **[Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC)](https://www.salesforce.com/products/commerce-cloud/)** — Enterprise omnichannel cloud platform tightly integrated with Salesforce CRM, service, and marketing clouds.
* **[SAP Commerce Cloud](https://www.sap.com/products/crm/e-commerce-platforms.html)** — Comprehensive B2B and B2C digital commerce suite deeply tied into SAP ERP environments.

## 📦 Lightweight & Digital Product Platforms

*Simple, low-friction checkout engines designed specifically for digital goods, subscriptions, and creator shops.*

* **[Lemon Squeezy](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/)** — Merchant of Record (MoR) platform handling global sales tax, VAT, licenses, and subscriptions for digital products and SaaS.
* **[Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/)** — Simple creator-focused digital download and course platform with built-in affiliate mechanics.
* **[Payhip](https://payhip.com/)** — Simple checkout platform for ebooks, software, digital downloads, and memberships with minimal setup.
* **[Sellfy](https://sellfy.com/)** — Turnkey store builder specifically tailored for print-on-demand and digital creators.
